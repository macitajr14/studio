# **App Name**: AgencyFlow

## Core Features:

- Logo Display: Display the agency logo prominently on the landing page.
- Service Description: Present a concise and compelling description of the marketing agency's services.
- Contact Form: Provide a clear and easy-to-use contact form for potential clients to reach out.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background: White or light gray for a clean, modern look.
- Text: Dark gray or black for high readability.
- Accent: A vibrant blue (#3498db) to highlight key elements such as buttons and links.
- Use a minimalist layout with plenty of white space to avoid clutter.
- Maintain a clean and professional look.
- Subtle animations on scroll or hover to add a touch of interactivity.
